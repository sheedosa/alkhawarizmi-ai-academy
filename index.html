OUR FOUNDATION

Heritage meets mission

Named after the mathematician whose work gave us the word algorithm.
OUR HERITAGE

Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi

Father of Algebra · Origin of the word “algorithm”

In the 9th century he wrote the book that gave algebra its name, working at the House of Wisdom in Baghdad. It set out how to solve a problem by following a fixed sequence of steps.

The word “algorithm” comes from his name. The step by step method he described is still how software solves problems today.

“We named the academy after him because we are trying to do the same thing here.”
780 to 850 CE Invented algebra Origin of “algorithm” House of Wisdom, Baghdad
OUR MISSION

For Libya. From Libya.

Dr. Ali Abusnina · Founder, AlKhwarizmi AI Academy

I spent years working with AI outside Libya, and kept meeting Libyans who wanted to learn it but had nowhere to go. That is why this academy exists.

We want these skills to reach every part of Libya, from Tripoli to Benghazi, in schools and in businesses. Learning them should not depend on being able to study abroad.

“From Libya, for Libya.”
