OUR FOUNDATION

Heritage meets mission

Inspired by the greatest mathematician of the medieval era and driven by a profound commitment to Libya's future.
OUR HERITAGE

Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi

Father of Algebra · Origin of the word “algorithm”

In the 9th century, al-Khwarizmi revolutionized mathematics by inventing algebra and introducing systematic problem-solving to the world. His landmark work laid the foundation for all modern computing.

His name gave birth to the word “algorithm” — the foundation of all computer science and artificial intelligence. Every AI model and intelligent system we use today traces back to his pioneering principles.

“Inspired by his timeless legacy, we are bringing AI education to our region.”

OUR MISSION

For Libya. From Libya.

Dr. Ali Abusnina · Founder, AlKhwarizmi AI Academy

This academy was born from a deep commitment to serve the Libyan people and our beloved nation. As a Libyan who has witnessed the transformative power of AI across the globe, I felt a calling to bring this knowledge home.

Our mission is to plant the seeds of AI adoption across Libya — from Tripoli to Benghazi, from our schools to our businesses, from our children to our executives. Every Libyan deserves access to world-class AI education.

“From Libya, for Libya — empowering our nation through AI excellence.”
