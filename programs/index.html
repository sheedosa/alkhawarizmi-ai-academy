HOW WE TEACH

The rules every hour

Across all seven tracks the teaching model is the same: short concept, long lab, one artefact that grows, and a published rubric you are measured against.
Labs are the lesson
Across the children's and public programmes, 58% of every session is hands-on. Learners operate AI far more than they watch it.
An eight-minute talking cap
Written into the instructor script as a hard rule. Nobody lectures for longer than that before the room does something.
Level, not age
Placement is a fifteen-minute practical activity, never a birth year. Mixed ages in one room is a feature, not a compromise.
Arabic first
Concepts are introduced in Arabic with the English term beside them, so learners discuss the work locally and read the documentation globally.
Evidence, not attendance
A rubric published before the course starts, a daily badge, a portfolio, and a certificate that names a level.
Works without the internet
Every lab has a documented offline route. A power cut or a dead connection never cancels a session.
