# AlKhwarizmi AI Academy — Website

Libya's first AI academy. Bilingual (English / العربية) site with full right-to-left support.

**Live site:** https://sheedosa.github.io/alkhawarizmi-ai-academy/

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, generated by a handful of Python scripts (kept outside the repository) and published as-is.
Build order: `python3 build_home.py && python3 seo_home.py && python3 assemble3.py && python3 localize.py`. The last step
writes the Arabic tree under `ar/`, `styles.min.css` and `sitemap.xml`.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Home |
| `ai-productivity.html`, `diploma.html` | Individuals route (register-interest forms). `studio.html` is a redirect stub kept for old links |
| `organisations.html` | Overview for companies and ministries, linked from the header, the footer and the Home table; the three programmes have their own pages (`strategy-week.html`, `custom-training.html`, `policy-briefing.html`), each with a consultation form `#consult` |
| `ar/` | The Arabic twin of every page above (same file names, Arabic first, `<html lang="ar" dir="rtl">`, Arabic titles, descriptions and structured data). Generated: edit the English source, not these |
| `about.html`, `insights.html`, `contact.html`, `privacy.html` | Academy pages |
| `404.html` | Shown for any unknown URL |
| `styles.css`, `styles.min.css` | Design tokens, sections, components, responsive + touch rules, Arabic typography resets. Pages load the minified copy; edit the readable one and rebuild |
| `fonts.css`, `assets/fonts/` | Self-hosted Aileron and Oxygen (Latin), Alexandria and Noto Naskh Arabic (Arabic), IBM Plex Mono for numerals (woff2 subsets). No Google Fonts request. |
| `main.js` | Language toggle, header dropdowns, mobile menu, lead forms, analytics events, Insights filter |
| `config.js` | **Fill this in:** Supabase URL + anon key, GA4 measurement id |
| `supabase/` | `001_leads.sql` (run once in your Supabase project) and setup notes |
| `assets/` | Logo (`mark.svg` vector mark used in the header, footer and favicon; `mark.png` fallback; `logo.png`), share image `og-logo.png`, the identity's line art in `brand/`, the al-Jabr figure in `motif/` (About only), drop-in photos in `photos/` |

## Language

Text the CSS cannot switch (dropdown options, form placeholders, meta description, aria labels)
carries `data-en` / `data-ar` (or `data-ph-en` / `data-ph-ar`) and is set by `main.js` on load and on toggle.
Number ranges inside Arabic text are wrapped in `<span class="num">` so they read left-to-right.


English is the default. The header toggle switches the whole page to Arabic (RTL) and the choice
persists in `localStorage` (`akaa-lang`). Every piece of copy exists twice in the markup:
`<span data-l="en">…</span><span data-l="ar">…</span>`; CSS shows the one matching `<html lang>`.

## Leads and analytics

- **Current backend: a Google Sheet.** Forms (`<form data-lead="…">`) POST to a Google Apps Script web app
  (`tools/sheet/Code.gs`, pasted into the sheet via Extensions > Apps Script) whose URL is `SHEET_URL` in
  `config.js`. Each enquiry becomes a row in the sheet's Enquiries tab (Status starts as New) and an alert email
  goes to info@alkhawarizmi.ai. The script drops honeypot and bot-fast submissions, limits floods and stores
  formula-like text as plain text. The sheet's "How it works" tab explains the columns and how to redeploy.
- **Later: Supabase.** With `SHEET_URL` empty and the Supabase keys set, forms write to the `leads` table instead
  (see `supabase/README.md`). With neither set, submitting opens a pre-filled email to info@alkhawarizmi.ai, so
  nothing is lost.
- GA4 loads only when `GA4_ID` is set. Events: `view_programme`, `select_route`, `cta_click`,
  `form_start`, `generate_lead` (mark as key event), `form_error`, `lang_toggle`, `filter_insights`.
  Roadmap and funnel definitions: `ROADMAP.md`.

## Design

An academy look modelled on a course-platform layout: light lavender and white pages with soft corner glows, cards
with 16px corners, and deep purple for the stats band, the AI Productivity feature, the closing panel and the footer. Purple is
the brand colour; amber (`--amber`) is the warm accent for secondary buttons, badges and the short bar before each
section label. Components read semantic variables (`--bg`, `--fg`, `--fg-soft`, `--muted`, `--rule`, `--link`,
`--surface`, `--card-border`, `--badge-bg`, `--btn-bg`), so a surface class only swaps values.

Type: headings in Aileron (800 for page titles, 600 and 700 below), text in Oxygen, numerals and chapter marks in IBM
Plex Mono. Arabic headings use Alexandria and Arabic text Noto Naskh Arabic (`size-adjust: 108%` keeps it the same visual
size as the Latin text), with more leading (1.75 to 1.85 for text) for the dots and vowel marks, and Arabic labels never
below 14.5px. All fonts are self-hosted in `assets/fonts/`; each tree preloads the three faces that paint above the fold
(English: Oxygen 400, Aileron 800, Aileron 600; Arabic: Noto Naskh 400, Alexandria 600, Alexandria 700). Headings carry
no closing full stop, and each page has one title in the reader's language.

Home runs: hero (centred title, two buttons, a row of programme frames with a stat card) · next groups · programme
catalogue with filter tabs · the academy (photo collage, badge, checklist) · who it's for (3 × 2 cards) · the academy in
who we are (hero) · video · the founder · why the academy (materials) · programme router · who teaches (when `TEAM` is filled) · numbers · enquiry
panel with a cut-out photo. Inner-page heroes are light with an at-a-glance card; a hero photo turns them dark.

Home is six sections: hero (two buttons that preset the programme filter) → the five programme cards with
All / Individuals / Organisations tabs → why us (four points and the numbers band) → how to join (three steps) →
four common questions → the enquiry form at `#enquire`, which every "enquire" link opens with the programme
preselected; a sticky bar offers it once the hero has scrolled away. The form posts to the Google Sheet like the
other forms (Form column = the chosen programme, or `contact` for "Not sure yet"; Page column = `/index.html#enquire`).
Nothing on Home repeats a programme page: the day-by-day plan, the nine documents and the teaching method live on
their own pages and on About.

Programme pages share one structure, ordered by the questions a visitor asks: hero with an at-a-glance card → who
it's for (one short passage) → what you do (AI Productivity's day-by-day plan, the Diploma's eight months, the Week's nine
documents, Custom Training's five steps) → what you leave with → editions → practical details → four questions (also
published as FAQPage data) → the form → other programmes as one line of links. The five programme pages are
`ai-productivity.html`, `diploma.html`, `strategy-week.html`, `custom-training.html` and `policy-briefing.html`;
`organisations.html` is a short overview with one consultation form for organisations that are not sure yet. Old links
to `organisations.html#week`, `#custom` and `#policy` redirect to the new pages. Practical details that are not known
yet are kept in `content.py` as `None` and stay off the page; `DETAILS.md` lists them for the academy to fill in.
Copy rules: headings of at most eight words, intros of at most two sentences, one call to action per section.

The About page tells the academy's story visually: an at-a-glance card, a four-step timeline from al-Khwarizmi to AI,
the site's figure explained in three drawn steps (how x² + 10x = 39 is solved by completing the square), the teaching
method as a session bar and icon tiles, a visit card with a drawn street map, the numbers band and a line of links to the programmes. A "Who teaches" section appears once `TEAM` in `content.py` has real people.

Brand: the site follows the 2026 visual identity. Colours are Emerald Teal `#09C98E`, Royal Indigo `#534CB6` and
Midnight Navy `#111128` (tokens `--teal`, `--indigo`/`--violet`, `--night-3`, gradient `--grad`); teal is never used for
small text on white (its contrast is too low), only for bars, buttons on navy, icons and large text on navy, with
`--teal-ink` for text. Fonts, all free and self-hosted in `assets/fonts/`: Aileron (CC0) for English headlines and
the wordmark, Oxygen (OFL) for English text, Alexandria (OFL, the nearest free face to the identity's Noor) for
Arabic headlines and the wordmark, Noto Naskh Arabic (OFL, a Naskh text face like Greta Arabic) for Arabic text.
`assets/brand/` holds the identity's devices as SVG: the geometric-expansion line art of the map, the K and the خ
(`expand-*.svg`, drawn behind navy sections), the dot marker (`dots.svg`, section openers) and the K pattern strip
(`k-pattern.svg`). The al-Jabr figure now appears only in the About story. The logo lockup is the mark beside the
two-line name (ALKHWARIZMI / AI ACADEMY, أكاديمية الخوارزمي / للذكاء الاصطناعي).

Search and sharing: every page has a title under 65 characters (Arabic under 70), a description under 160 (the build
refuses longer ones), a canonical URL, Open Graph and X/Twitter tags, `hreflang` links to its twin in the other language
(with `x-default` on the English page), and structured data (the organisation, with opening hours, and the website on
Home; Course with its schedule, stated hours and certificate, FAQPage and BreadcrumbList on programme pages; an ItemList
on the Organisations overview; BreadcrumbList on the rest). The Arabic twins carry the Arabic names, questions and
breadcrumbs. `sitemap.xml` lists both trees with alternates, and `lastmod` comes from git. Links shared on WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn or X show `og-logo.png`, the
logo on white (1200×630). If a platform still shows an old preview, re-scrape the URL in its debugger (Facebook Sharing
Debugger, LinkedIn Post Inspector). Submit `sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console: on a `github.io` project address
`robots.txt` is not read from this folder, so the sitemap must be submitted by hand until the site has its own domain.

Social badges: the academy's accounts in `config.js` (`SOCIAL`) appear as outlined icon badges in the footer and on
About, and as icon-and-name pills on Contact; `main.js` renders them only for accounts with a real `https://` link and
adds the same links as `sameAs` on the organisation's structured data. Empty accounts are not shown.

Analytics: every button and link carries a `data-track` event (`select_route`, `cta_click`, `filter_programmes`,
`form_start`, `generate_lead`). They are sent to GA4 once `GA4_ID` is set in `config.js`; mark `generate_lead` as a
key event in GA4 to measure enquiries.

Content lives in the scratchpad generators: `content.py` holds the next-group dates (`NEXT_GROUPS`), the numbers
(`STATS`), the FAQ, and `TESTIMONIALS` / `PARTNERS`. The testimonials section and the partner logos stay hidden
until those lists contain real quotes and real partners. Every number on the site is a real programme fact.

The recurring figure (`assets/motif/`) is al-Khwarizmi's own: the completing-the-square diagram from his book of
algebra, which solves x² + 10x = 39. Programme drawings (`icons.py`) use the same 1.5px line.

## Photos

Every image slot is a `<figure data-photo="id">`. Upload the original `assets/photos/<id>.jpg` (4K welcome): the
`Photos` workflow (`.github/workflows/photos.yml`, script `tools/photos.mjs`, using sharp) writes WebP sizes from
640 to 3840 px to `assets/photos/web/` and lists them in `assets/photos/manifest.json`. `main.js` reads the manifest
and gives each slot a `srcset`, so browsers download only the width they need; ids not in the manifest make no
request. Bands and programme images show a framed placeholder while empty (a light frame where a dark one would merge with a neighbouring purple section); other slots stay hidden. The full list
of slots, file names and sizes is in `PHOTOS.md`. Add `?photos` to any URL to see each slot labelled.

## Language

The English page lives at the root and the Arabic page under `ar/` with the same file name. Every page's own `lang`
matches its URL; the inline script in the head sends a visitor to the twin when their saved choice (`akaa-lang`) is the
other language, or on a first visit from an Arabic browser. The toggle saves the choice and goes to the twin. Both
trees keep the `data-l` spans, so one stylesheet and one script serve both; `404.html` (served at any path) switches in
place. `localize.py` derives the Arabic page from the finished English one: it swaps the head, prefixes asset paths with
`../`, localises the structured data and adds the hreflang links to both files.

## Deployment

Served via GitHub Pages from the `main` branch root. `404.html` uses `<base href="/alkhawarizmi-ai-academy/">`
so it renders correctly at any depth; change it if the site moves to a custom domain.

Home, founder and video: `FOUNDER` (with its `bio` paragraph) and `FOUNDER_PLACES` in `content.py` feed the founder
section on Home and About (`founder_block()` in `progblocks.py`); the biography and the "Where he has worked" badges
render only when filled in. Every form shows the three "what happens next" steps from `JOIN` (`next_steps()`), and until
a cohort date is set the pages say "Not yet scheduled. Register and you hear first" and promise a reply within two
working days. The Contact form carries a programme select; `contact.html#diploma` (or `#unsure`) preselects it. The
Privacy page describes analytics only while a GA4 id is configured.
`VIDEO_URL` in `config.js` (YouTube or Vimeo page link) turns the Home video poster into a player; empty keeps the poster
and the "Video coming soon" pill. The Studio was renamed AI Productivity: the page is `ai-productivity.html`, the internal
slug and lead value stay `studio` (sheet and database unchanged), and `studio.html` is a hand-written redirect stub.

## House style for copy

Checked by `copylint.py` after every build (the build fails on a finding). Second person to the reader, "we" for the
academy; present tense for descriptions, "will" for promises. Contractions only in headings and labels; body copy
uncontracted. "Dr Ali Abusnina" without a full stop. Programme names: AI Productivity; the Diploma (full name "the AI
Practitioner Diploma" on first mention); the AI Strategy Week, then "the Strategy Week"; Custom Training; the Policy
Briefing. Editions: "Open edition", "Open week". "Organisations" is the category word; "companies and ministries" at
most once per page as its gloss. Numerals with units and in specs, tables and bars; words up to twelve in headings and
prose; never start a line with a numeral. Prices "7,500 LYD per person" (individuals) and "10,200 LYD per participant"
(organisations). One verb per audience: "Register interest", "Request a free consultation", "Write to us"; the Home form
says "Send an enquiry". "Within two working days" at most twice per page (the steps and the thank-you); "free
consultation" at most eight times; no exclamation marks, no hype words, no American spellings, no heading ending in a
full stop, no sentence over 32 words. Optional fields say "(optional)"; the rest are required. Each programme page
answers its own questions (price, dates, language) rather than listing every programme. Every form shows the three
"what happens next" steps for its audience (`JOIN` in `content.py`), and the empty states are honest: "Not yet
scheduled", "Film coming soon", "The first pieces are being written".

Design restraint (October 2026): section headings stand alone, with no small label above them. One brand device per
section at most (the dot marker only on openers marked `sec-head--mark`, the K strip above the footer, the gradient bar on
the footer). No glow backgrounds, frosted panels, pill tags, round arrow buttons, hover lifts or counting numbers. Links
carry no arrow characters.

## The idea behind the design: completing the square

The academy is named after al-Khwarizmi, whose *al-Jabr* solved x² + 10x = 39 with a drawing: a square completed
by four shaded corners. That drawing is the site's one visual signature. It is generated by `motif.py` in the build
scratchpad (`drawn()` for the animated version, `faint()` for the quiet one on navy) and appears in exactly four places:
the Home hero, where it draws itself stroke by stroke on load; the Home enquiry panel and the video poster, faint and
still; the About page, where it illustrates the three steps of the method; and the 404 page. Nowhere else.

Everything else is type and line: chapter numerals in IBM Plex Mono (`01`–`06` on Home), hairline rules instead of
boxed cards, one ruled table for the five programmes, four large figures for the numbers, white pages with navy only
for the interior heroes, the Home enquiry panel, the consultation forms and the footer. Rules worth keeping:

- No stock or generated imagery. Photo slots (`PHOTOS.md`) stay hidden until a real photograph exists.
- No boxes around text: separate things with a rule, a numeral or white space.
- Motion is limited to the square drawing itself and chapter openers settling in once (`.reveal`); both are off under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`, and everything is visible without JavaScript (`html.has-js` gates the hidden states).
- Digits in mono labels stay Latin in Arabic (`.num`).
